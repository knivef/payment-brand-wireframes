Why move

Most collection rails were not built for this book

Your average ticket and your largest ticket are an order of magnitude apart, your clients are spread across jurisdictions, and your provider priced the relationship for neither.

1

Ticket ceiling

A rail that caps at a few thousand quietly loses your best funding clients. They do not complain, they just stop.

2

Settlement that does not match treasury

You hold in crypto, they settle in fiat, or the reverse. Every cycle becomes a conversion exercise.

3

Terms that move after integration

Rate up, reserve up, settlement slower. The integration cost is what keeps you there.

FAQ

Can't find your answer? Talk to our team.

Who is AltPay built for?
Forex and CFD brokers, crypto businesses, and affiliate networks that need to collect client deposits by bank transfer or card and settle in crypto.
What is the minimum and maximum deposit?
Deposits start at $250 and a single deposit can go up to $100,000.
Which payment networks do you accept?
All the major ones.
  • Bank transfers: SWIFT, SEPA, SEPA Instant, ACH, Fedwire, UK Faster Payments and CHAPS.
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard.
  • Crypto settlement: USDT and USDC on Ethereum, Tron, Solana, Polygon and BNB Chain.
What does whitelisted mean?
Payments go through pre-approved, trusted channels, which means smoother and more reliable processing.
How do I receive settlement?
Funds are settled to your wallet in crypto: USDT or USDC on Ethereum, Tron, Solana, Polygon or BNB Chain.
How long does onboarding take?
Onboarding is completed in 5 to 10 business days.
What verification do I need?
It depends on the transaction type and the amount.
  • Fiat to crypto: KYC required.
  • *Crypto to fiat: no KYC required for transactions up to $50,000. For higher limits, KYC needs to be completed.
  • KYC for individuals: government-issued photo ID and proof of address.
  • KYB for businesses: invoices and incorporation certificates, or UBO details.
