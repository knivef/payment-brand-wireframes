Why move

Most collection rails were not built for this book

Your average ticket and your largest ticket are an order of magnitude apart, your clients are spread across jurisdictions, and your provider priced the relationship for neither.

1

Ticket ceiling

A rail that caps at a few thousand quietly loses your best funding clients. They do not complain, they just stop.

2

Settlement that does not match treasury

You hold in crypto, they settle in fiat, or the reverse. Every cycle becomes a conversion exercise.

3

Terms that move after integration

Rate up, reserve up, settlement slower. The integration cost is what keeps you there.
