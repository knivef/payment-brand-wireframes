The gap

Holding stablecoins is not the same as being paid

Revenue arrives on chain. Payroll, suppliers and tax do not accept it. The exchange that would convert it asks for source of funds every time you withdraw at scale, and your bank asks a different set of questions when the wire lands.

01

The exchange route breaks at volume

Retail exchange withdrawal limits and repeated source-of-funds reviews are not a treasury process. They are a queue you join every month.

02

Your bank does not know what the wire is

A credit with no clear originator and no settlement reference is the kind of transaction that gets held while someone asks you about it.

03

The rate is discovered afterwards

Most providers quote a headline fee and recover the real margin in the spread. You find out what you actually paid when you reconcile.

Questions we actually get

The ones that come up on the first call.

Which list will I be on, low risk or high risk?
Decided at onboarding from your industry, your corridor and your entity structure. We tell you before you sign, not after. Low risk is 1.50 percent either direction with no setup and no monthly fee. High risk is 2.50 percent with a $250 monthly maintenance fee.
Do you hold a rolling reserve?
No. Neither offramp list carries a reserve. That is the main structural difference between this and a card acquiring relationship.
What is the minimum and maximum I can send?
Low risk runs from $1,000 to $1,000,000 per ticket. High risk runs from $1,000 to $500,000. Larger tickets are possible but need to be arranged in advance.
How is FX handled?
Conversion is charged at 0.15 percent over the exchange rate, plus the bank's actual FX on the fiat leg. You see the rate before you confirm the transaction.
Which chains and assets?
USDT and USDC on the major chains, plus BTC and ETH. If you need a chain that is not supported, ask before you send.
How fast does fiat arrive?
Conversion is same day in most cases. Bank arrival depends on the corridor. SEPA is typically same or next business day, SWIFT is two to three.
What do you need from me to open an account?
Low risk is a one step signup with entity details and the destination bank account. High risk adds a short KYB on the entity and its beneficial owners.
