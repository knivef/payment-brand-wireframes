The usual story

Crypto cards hide the real number in three places

The headline is the transaction fee. The cost is the transaction fee plus the deposit fee plus the FX markup, and only one of those is usually on the page.

1

The deposit fee nobody mentions

You get charged to load the card, before you have spent anything. On a 5 percent tier that is money gone at top up.

2

FX on every non-USD purchase

A separate markup on top of the network fee, applied every time you spend outside your base currency.

3

Tier requirements found after signup

The good rate turns out to need a monthly spend you were never told about.

Documents

What we need, exactly

Entity documents

Certificate of incorporation and the share register. Standard, and usually the quick part.

UBO identification

ID front and back, photographed. Scanned copies are not accepted, which is the single most common reason an application gets sent back.

Proof of address

For the UBO, photographed not scanned, and dated within the last three months. Older than three months and it will be rejected.

Card questions

Asked before every application.

Which tier is actually cheapest for me?
Work it out from your monthly spend. Below 5K, Silver at 5 percent is your only option. Above 5K, Gold at 3 percent. Above 25K, Platinum at 2.50 percent. Tell us your real number and we will confirm.
What is the all-in percentage made of?
Deposit fee charged when you load, transaction fee charged when you spend, and non-USD FX charged on any purchase outside USD. All three are published above.
Does the 2 percent FX include the network fee?
Yes. The 2.00 percent non-USD figure includes the 1.00 percent Visa fee. It is not charged on top.
Why are scanned documents rejected?
Photographed copies only, for ID and proof of address. It is the most common reason an application gets sent back, which is why it is on this page rather than in an email after you apply.
How old can my proof of address be?
Three months. Anything older is rejected, no exceptions.
What does it cost to get started?
$199 setup and $49 per month, on every tier.
How do I get on the Black tier?
50K a month spend and approval. It is not self-serve, and we will tell you plainly if the volume is not there yet.
