The mechanism

Published reserve terms, on one balance you can read

A reserve is not unreasonable in these verticals. Chargeback liability windows in the card schemes run for months after the sale, and any acquirer carrying that risk holds something back. What is unreasonable is finding out the size of it after you have signed. We publish the percentage and the release schedule up front, and because card settlement and the crypto offramp run on the same ledger rather than through a third-party partner, what is held and when it releases is a line on your balance with a date against it, not a number you have to ask an account manager for.

What is your rolling reserve, and when does it release?
We hold [PERCENTAGE] of each transaction and release it on [SCHEDULE]. Both numbers are on this page and in your terms before you apply. If your reserve can change, the trigger conditions for that change are written into your agreement, so a reserve increase is something you can see coming rather than something you discover in a payout.
What happens to my held funds if you terminate my account?
Your exit terms state what is held, for how long after your last transaction, and the date it releases. [EXIT TERMS DETAIL.] Chargeback liability windows in the card schemes run for months after the sale, so no acquirer releases everything on day one. The difference is that you will have read our schedule before you signed rather than after.
Do you actually approve iGaming and adult, or do you approve and then reserve me into leaving?
