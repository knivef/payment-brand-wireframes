Why brokers move

The collection rail is usually the weakest part of the business

Deposits convert the client. Withdrawals keep them. Most brokers are running both through something that was never designed for the volume, the ticket size, or the questions a bank asks.

I

Ticket ceilings that cut off your best clients

A rail built for $2,000 deposits quietly loses you the $80,000 one. The client does not complain, they just stop funding.

II

Withdrawals that take longer than the pitch promised

Payout speed is the single most reviewed thing about a broker. It is also the thing most providers treat as a batch job.

III

Terms that change once you are integrated

The reserve moves, the rate moves, the settlement schedule stretches. By then the integration cost makes leaving expensive.

Diligence

What we ask for, and what we do with it

What we ask for

Certificate of incorporation, ownership structure, UBO identification, your licence where the jurisdiction requires one, and six months of processing history if you have it.

What we run

KYB on the entity, sanctions and PEP screening on beneficial owners, wallet screening on crypto legs, and transaction monitoring sized to the book.

What we will not do

Before you ask us on the call

The questions that decide whether this is a fit.

What is the largest single deposit you can take?
$150,000 on both lists. That ceiling is why brokers with a VIP book come to us, because most retail-built rails stop well below it.
What is the reserve and when is it released?
5 percent released over 90 days on the VIP list. 10 percent over 180 days on the high risk list. Which applies is on your term sheet before you sign.
Do I need a licence?
Not always, but it changes which list you land on and what the reserve looks like. Tell us the real position in the application. Hiding it costs you more time than disclosing it.
Can I settle in crypto instead of fiat?
Yes. Crypto settlement runs off the same account at the rate shown on the list. Plenty of brokers take fiat deposits and settle in stablecoin.
How is FX charged?
0.15 percent over the exchange rate plus the bank's actual FX. It is a line on the rate card, not a spread recovered quietly.
What is the qualifying volume for the VIP list?
$250,000 a month equivalent. The high risk list has a $100,000 minimum. Below that, tell us anyway and we will say whether to come back later.
