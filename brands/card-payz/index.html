Questions merchants ask

Straight answers before you apply.

What counts as high risk?
Any business card networks flag for chargebacks, regulation or reputation: iGaming, adult, nutra, peptides, dating, VPN, subscriptions and more.
Can I get approved after a termination or MATCH listing?
Often. Disclose it upfront.
How much does it cost?
Setup from $1,500, $149 a month, rate quoted after underwriting.
Is there a rolling reserve?
Most high-risk accounts carry one. Yours is stated on the term sheet before you sign.
Do you take US merchants?
Yes, through the US programme.
Do you offer crypto?
