What actually ends accounts

The line moved in April and nobody made your processor tell you

Approval is a single day. The ratio is every day after it, and most merchants in these verticals find out where they stand from a penalty rather than from their acquirer.

1

The line moved and nobody called you

On 1 April 2026 the Visa excessive threshold fell from 2.2 percent to 1.5 percent, a 32 percent tightening in one step, with no warning tier and 8 dollars per disputed transaction. Merchants who were compliant in March were in breach in April without changing anything.

2

Your vertical is already above the line

iGaming chargebacks typically run 2 to 4 percent against an ecommerce baseline of 0.5 to 1 percent. Nutra continuity offers and adult rebills land in the same place by a different route.

3

Nobody told you the threshold

Dispute thresholds are routinely left out of the conversation before signing, so merchants find out where the line is from an unexpected email. Of the six providers we tore down, none publishes a rate, a reserve, a chargeback fee, or the threshold that governs the account.

Verticals

Four verticals, four underwriting paths, not one product

A

iGaming

MCC 7995, manually underwritten, and your licence jurisdiction decides the acquirer. UKGC, MGA, DGOJ and US state licences, plus Curacao, Gibraltar and Isle of Man operators. [WHICH JURISDICTIONS WE CAN ACTUALLY BOARD.] The ratio matters most here, because 2 to 4 percent is normal and the line is 1.5.

B

Adult

MCC 5967, and 7273 for dating. The account is usually lost to the descriptor rather than the product: a charge a subscriber will not explain on a shared statement becomes a dispute rather than a refund request. Descriptor control and rebill hygiene are the work.

C

Peptides

A restricted category under Mastercard BRAM, gated on research-use-only positioning and a site review. This is the hardest of the four and the volume floor is real. [MINIMUM MONTHLY VOLUME WE CAN BOARD FOR PEPTIDES.] If we cannot board you we will say so on the first call.

D

Nutra

MCC 5122 and 5499, continuity billing. Underwriters read the ads, not just the application, and a funnel with a disease claim or a fake countdown will sink an otherwise clean file. Trial to rebill is where the disputes come from.

These are four different underwriting paths, not one product. If you run two of them under one company, say so early, because they will not sit behind the same acquirer.

What operators ask before they move acquirer

Including the two where the answer is conditional rather than yes.

What is the VAMP ratio and what is the threshold?
It is your reported fraud plus your disputes, divided by your settled transactions. Visa combined the old dispute and fraud monitoring programmes into that single number. Since 1 April 2026 the merchant excessive threshold is 1.5 percent, down from 2.2 percent, with no warning tier and 8 dollars per disputed or fraudulent transaction. Merchants who were compliant in March were in breach in April without changing anything.
My chargebacks are already above 1.5 percent. Can you still board me?
Possibly, and being above the line is a reason to talk rather than a disqualification, but it is conditional and we will not pretend otherwise. What matters is where the disputes come from and whether the levers that move the ratio are available on your traffic. We will tell you on the first call whether we think we can get you under the line and how long it would take.
Who actually files the representments, you or me?
We do. We take the pre-dispute alert, decide whether refunding is cheaper than fighting it, and file the representment with the evidence pack. Most of this category states that a dispute process exists without saying who performs it, and in practice that usually means a portal login and your time.
Can you reprice me in month four?
Only on the triggers written into your agreement, and those triggers are in there before you sign. The pattern merchants complain about in this category is that processors move immediately when chargebacks spike but never move back when they fall. If your ratio improves, the agreement says what happens.
What is your rolling reserve and when does it release?
[PERCENTAGE] held, released [SCHEDULE], and the conditions that can change it are in the agreement. For context, the published category norm is 5 to 10 percent held for 90 to 180 days, and most providers do not state theirs anywhere before you sign.
Do you take iGaming, and which licences?
Yes, under MCC 7995, which is manually underwritten with no automated approval. Your licence jurisdiction decides which acquirer you sit behind. [WHICH JURISDICTIONS WE CAN ACTUALLY BOARD.] If your licence does not match the market you are selling into, that is usually the real reason an application fails.
Do you take peptides?
Conditionally, and this is the hardest of our four verticals. It is a restricted category under Mastercard's BRAM programme, it is gated on research-use-only positioning and a site review, and there is a real volume floor. [MINIMUM MONTHLY VOLUME WE CAN BOARD FOR PEPTIDES.] If your site carries human-use or dosage claims, no acquirer will hold the account regardless of who signs you.
What happens if your acquirer exits my vertical?
It happens, and recently: one provider wound down its UK operation in late 2025. Losing an account stops deposits immediately, locks your reserve, and can put you on the MATCH list for five years. Ask us what our position is before you sign, and we will tell you what redundancy exists on your vertical rather than claiming it never happens.
