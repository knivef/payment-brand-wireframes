The mechanism

Why there is no application

Card processing is a permission. An acquiring bank underwrites you, and in this category it will not underwrite a research-only store under six figures a month, which is why the quotes you have been getting start where they do. That is the whole reason a store doing twenty thousand a month can start today, and it is also why we will not pretend to offer you cards.

Questions people actually ask before signing up

Including the two we lose on, answered first rather than buried.

Do I need a minimum monthly volume?
No. There is no floor, because there is no acquiring bank setting one. A store doing twenty thousand a month is treated the same as one doing two hundred thousand. This is the single biggest difference between us and a card merchant account, where the published targets in this category start at a hundred thousand a month.
Is there an application fee or a setup fee?
No. Nothing to apply, nothing to set up, nothing monthly, and nothing to leave. The only thing you pay is [RATE] on money you have actually received. In this category the published entry costs run to five hundred dollars to apply, fifty to three hundred to set up and two to five hundred to leave, all before anyone tells you whether you qualify.
Can my customers pay by card?
No, and that is the honest limitation of what we do. FiatPay is crypto to fiat settlement. Your customer pays in stablecoin and you receive fiat. If accepting Visa and Mastercard at checkout is the thing you need, we are not it, and you should read the next answer before you spend money trying.
Why can I not get a card merchant account for peptides?
Because card networks classify research chemicals under restricted categories and Mastercard's BRAM programme targets this one specifically. In practice a research-use-only seller is told he needs a proven six-figure monthly processing history before cards are discussed, and one specialist quoted in the trade press requires half a million a month. It is not about your business being bad. It is about your business being small, and nobody in this category says so before they invoice you.
Do I need LegitScript certification?
Not for this. LegitScript is enforced by Visa and Mastercard under their internet pharmacy programmes and applies only to card-based processing, so it does not apply to settlement. For the card path it is a real cost: one provider publishes it at 1,075 dollars a year.
How do I get the money, and in what currency?
