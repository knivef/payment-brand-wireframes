### Patterns borrowed from category leaders

- **BVNK:** leads with reliability ("Unlock reliable fiat payments"), puts trust numbers right under the hero, repeats one CTA ("Speak to an expert") everywhere.
- **Radom:** payouts hero is a plain promise ("Global payouts, both crypto and fiat"), lists use cases by payee type (affiliates, creators, contractors), FAQ answers cost and funding questions directly.
- **Corepay:** names the enemy ("built for the clinics Stripe and Square shut down"), states who they will NOT serve, adds a savings calculator, lists exactly what you need to apply.
- **SoarPay / HighRiskPay:** comparison table against mainstream and broker alternatives, vertical grid feeding dedicated landing pages, approval-time promise near the CTA.

### Shared conversion rules for all six sites

1. One primary CTA per site, repeated in nav, hero, mid-page and footer. One secondary CTA (Telegram, since TG is the lead channel on every brand).
2. Show real numbers from the price list. Transparent fees are the strongest differentiator in a category where nobody publishes pricing.
3. Every hero has microcopy under the CTA that removes one fear (time, cost, rejection).
4. Short application form: 5 fields max on the homepage, full KYB later.
5. No invented proof. No merchant counts, volumes or testimonials until they are real.
6. Never say "no KYC", "licensed" or "MiCA compliant" on brands with Licence = None. Use "settled through licensed partners".
7. FAQ section on every page, marked up with FAQPage schema.
